How to play

Beat the dealer without going over 21. Set your bet and deal. Hit takes a card, Stand keeps your hand, Double doubles your bet for exactly one more card, and Split plays a pair as two hands for a second bet. On a keyboard, Space deals; H, S, D and P hit, stand, double and split; I and N take or refuse insurance.

Blackjack pays 3:2, other wins 1:1, and a tie returns your bet. The dealer stands on all 17s and checks for blackjack before you play; against an ace, insurance costs half your bet and pays 2:1. Double on any first two cards, split a pair once, and split aces take one card each; 21 after a split pays 1:1. No surrender. The deck is unlimited: every card is a fresh draw from all 52.

With the best play, the house edge is 0.57% of your initial bet.
